Overview

OTTO is a wheeled bipedal robot built at the Institute of Field Robotics (FIBO). It rolls on two wheels like a balancing robot and uses its two-joint legs to change height and leave the ground.

This project covers its dynamics and control, split into four topics: the somersault, the baseline controllers with the jump, the real-to-sim identification and the reinforcement learning policies. It is my bachelor thesis, with Dr. Kitti Thamrongaphichartkul and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Supachai Vongbunyong as advisors. Earlier student teams at FIBO studied the robot before me, and the somersault paper is submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.

Topics

Somersault

A full forward somersault planned as one optimization and tracked with NMPC in simulation

Baseline Controllers

Balancing, driving, height, roll leveling and the jump on the physical robot

Real-to-Sim

Identifying the motors, mass and sensor offsets so the simulation behaves like the robot

Reinforcement Learning

Stand-up and kneel policies trained around an identified model of the robot
